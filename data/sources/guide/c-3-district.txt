C-3: Neighborhood Business District

 The purpose of the C-3 Neighborhood Business District is to provide a range of shopping, goods and convenience services that cater largely to residents in the surrounding neighborhood. Businesses in the new district will be small in scale and of a lower intensity than those permitted in the C-1 and C-2 Districts.
 The C-3 Neighborhood Business District permits a maximum building height of four (4) residential floors over one (1) grade-level commercial floor that can not exceed 52 feet above grade. For residential floors, a minimum floor-to-floor height of 10 feet is required. For commercial floors, a minimum floor-to-floor height of 12 feet is required. Maximum lot coverage permitted is 60%. A minimum rear setback of 4 0% of the lot is required, while no front or side yard setbacks are required. The maximum residential density permitted within this district is calculated by dividing the lot area by 500. O ff-street parking regulations are specified by Use in Section-196-G .  Note: Properties within the C-3 District that are also located within an Historic District Overlay (HDO) are subject to additional or different bulk requirements. To check if your property is within an HDO, visit the City's Zoning Map . To learn more about the rules and regulations for historic properties in Hoboken, visit the Historic Preservation Chapter of the City's Municipal Code.
