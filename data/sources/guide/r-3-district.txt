R-3 District

 The purpose of this district is: 
 to advance the achievement of a viable residential neighborhood; 
 to encourage conservation and rehabilitation of existing sound residential blocks; 
 to support residential revitalization by a variety of housing types and related uses; and 
 to otherwise reinforce the residential characteristics of this district by regulating uses and structures not compatible with district objectives. 
 The R-3 District permits a maximum residential building height of 40 feet above Design Flood Elevation (DFE). A minimum floor-to-floor ceiling height of 10 feet is required. Lots within this district must be 25 feet wide. A minimum rear setback of 30 feet, or 30% of lot is required. The side yard setback must be either 0 feet or 5 feet. No front yard setback is required, except where prevailing setbacks apply. In such cases, the front yard prevailing setback can not exceed 10 feet. The number of residential units (i.e., density) allowed is determined by dividing the site area by 660 and rounding to the closest whole number. No off-street parking is required for the first five dwelling units. At least one space for each dwelling unit after the first five is required, except as modified in Article XI .
