C-2: Central Business District

 The purpose of the C-2 Central Business District is to promote and maintain Hoboken's thriving Main Street and core downtown shopping district. This includes the expansion of the previously designated Central Business District, adopting new uses not previously recognized, and providing specific guidance to efficiently facilitate the approval process for new businesses. See Figure 1 for an example of development permitted within the C-2 District. 
 The C-2 Central Business District bulk regulations effectively contain two subsets of regulations for properties depending upon whether or not the property abuts Court Street along its rear property line. 

 C-2 District 
 For properties within the C-2 District that do not abut Court Street, a maximum principal building height of 52 feet above average adjacent grade is permitted. Ground-floor commercial with residential above is required for principal buildings. For residential floors, a minimum floor-to-floor height of 10 feet is required. For commercial floors, a minimum floor-to-floor height of 12 feet is required. Maximum lot coverage permitted for principal buildings fronting Washington Street is 60%. A minimum rear setback of 4 0% of the lot is required, while no side yard setback is required. Additionally, n o front yard setback is required, except where front yard prevailing setbacks apply. The maximum residential density permitted within this district is calculated by dividing the lot area by 330. O ff-street parking regulations are specified by Use in Section-196-G . See Figure 2 for an illustration of the C-2 District Bulk Requirements. 
 Note: Properties within the C-2 District that are also located within an Historic District Overlay (HDO) are subject to additional or different bulk requirements. To check if your property is within an HDO, visit the City's Zoning Map . To learn more about the rules and regulations for historic properties in Hoboken, visit the Historic Preservation Chapter of the City's Municipal Code.

 C-2 District (Court Street) 
 For properties within the C-2 District that abut Court Street, in addition to a mixed-use principal building fronting Washington Street, an accessory buildings with grade-level parking and upper-level residential floors is permitted in the rear along Court Street. The maximum height permitted for maximum principal building height of 52 feet above average adjacent grade is permitted, while an accessory building height of 30 feet above grade is permitted. For residential floors, a minimum floor-to-floor height of 10 feet is required. For commercial floors, a minimum floor-to-floor height of 12 feet is required. Maximum lot coverage for principal buildings fronting Washington Street is 60%, while the maximum lot coverage for accessory buildings fronting Court Street is 20%. For property abutting Court Street, the maximum residential density is calculated by dividing the lot area by 330 and adding one (1) unit. O ff-street parking regulations are specified by Use in Section-196-G . See Figures 3 and 4 for an illustration of the C-2 Court Street District Bulk Requirements.
