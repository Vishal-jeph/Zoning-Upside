R-1 District

 The purpose of this district is:
 to conserve the architecture, scale and grain of residential blocks and street patterns; 
 to reinforce the residential character of the district;
 to acknowledge the traditional relationship between Stevens Institute, adjacent residential neighborhoods and neighborhood retail businesses and services; and
 to regulate those buildings and activities not comparable with district objectives.
 The R-1 District permits a maximum residential building height of 40 feet above Design Flood Elevation (DFE), or 3 stories. A minimum floor-to-floor ceiling height of 10 feet is required. T he building must have a 30-foot, or 30% of lot, rear setback at minimum. No side or front yard setbacks are required, except where front yard prevailing setbacks apply. In such cases, the front yard prevailing setback can not exceed 10 feet. The number of residential units (i.e., density) permitted is determined by dividing the site area by 660 and rounding to the closest whole number. No off-street parking is permitted. See Figure 1 for an illustration of the R-1 District Bulk Requirements. 
 Note: Properties within the R-1 District that are also located within an Historic District Overlay (HDO) are subject to additional or different bulk requirements. To check if your property is within an HDO, visit the City's Zoning Map . To learn more about the rules and regulations for historic properties in Hoboken, visit the City's Historic Preservation Chapter of the Municipal Code. 

 R-1 District Bulk Requirements: 
 Lot area (min.): 2,000 sf
 Lot width (min.): 100 ft.
 Lot depth (min.): 100 ft.
 Lot coverage (max.): 60%
 Building Height: 40 ft. above DFE, or three stories
 Min. floor-to-floor Height: 10 ft.
 Front yard: 0 ft.
 Rear yard: 30 ft. or 30% of lot depth
 Side yard: 0 ft.
 Density (min.): 660 square feet per dwelling unit
 Density (max.): Site area divided by 660
