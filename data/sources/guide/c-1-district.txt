C-1 Hoboken Terminal District

 The purpose of the C-1 Hoboken Terminal District is to promote economic job growth and a healthy business environment closest to New Jersey's second busiest transit hub. The high density of businesses, entertainment, jobs, and recreational and social opportunities within the compact terminal district serve to increase transit ridership and decrease automobile dependency and automobile-related congestion and pollution; all core principles of transit-oriented development. The C-1 District is the City's gateway for residents and visitors entering Hoboken by mass transit.
 The C-1 Hoboken Terminal District permits a maximum commercial building height of 60 feet above average adjacent grade , or 5 stories. A minimum floor-to-floor ceiling height of 10 feet is required. For new construction or an expansion of the existing structure, a minimum rear setback of 2 0% of the lot is required. For new construction, a minimum side yard setback of 0 feet or 5 feet is required. However, n o front yard setback is required, except where front yard prevailing setbacks apply. The residential density permitted within this district is set in accordance with Use conditions in Section-196-G . Similarly, the number of o ff-street parking spaces permitted is specified by Use in Section-196-G . 
 The C-1 Hoboken Terminal District permits a maximum lot coverage of 80% on the ground floor and 60% on upper floors. Though related, the maximum lot coverage is not the same as the required rear yard setback. For example, on a lot that's 100 feet deep, upper floors could partially be less than 40 feet from the rear lot line, as long as the overall coverage is 60% or less. On the ground floor, the rear yard setback is 20% of the lot for expansions and new construction, which would take up the remainder of the lot if the building reaches its maximum coverage of 80%.  Note: Properties within the C-1 District that are also located within an Historic District Overlay (HDO) are subject to additional or different bulk requirements. To check if your property is within an HDO, visit the City's Zoning Map . To learn more about the rules and regulations for historic properties in Hoboken, visit the Historic Preservation Chapter of the City's Municipal Code.
