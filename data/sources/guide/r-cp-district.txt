R-CP (Castle Point) District

 The purpose of the R-CP District is to reinforce and safeguard the heritage of this residential area which is one of the oldest and most prestigious neighborhoods in Hoboken. This district is distinguished by being the only neighborhood in the City typified by large, freestanding and semi-attached one- and two-family homes. Through architectural and bulk controls, the height, density, and uses will be limited to maintain Castle Point's scale and historic character. See Figure 1 for an example of the type of development permitted in the R-CP District.
 The R-CP district permits a maximum residential building height of 40 feet above average adjacent grade , or 3.5 stories. The bottom-most story would then be partially above and partially below grade; the portion below grade would not be counted towards the maximum height. There are no floor-to-floor ceiling height requirements for buildings within this district. A minimum rear setback of 30-foot, or 30% of lot is required. On at least one side, a minimum 5-foot side yard setback is required. No front yard setback is required, except where front yard prevailing setbacks apply. In such cases, the front yard prevailing setback can not exceed 10 feet. The number of residential units (i.e., density) allowed is determined by dividing the site area by 2,000 and rounding to the closest whole number and calculated to the thousandth place. No off-street parking is permitted or required, except for accessory garages pre-dating Ord. B-219 (1/15/2020). See Figure 2 for an illustration of the R-1 District Bulk Requirements. 
 Note: Properties within the R-CP District that are also located within an Historic District Overlay (HDO) are subject to additional or different bulk requirements. To check if your property is within an HDO, visit the City's Zoning Map . To learn more about the rules and regulations for historic properties in Hoboken, visit the Historic Preservation Chapter of the City's Municipal Code.
