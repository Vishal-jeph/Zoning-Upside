R-2 District

 The purpose of this district is:
 to encourage neighborhood stability through conservation and rehabilitation of residential structures;
 to facilitate conversion of nonresidential to residential space; and 
 to otherwise reinforce the residential characteristics of this district by restricting uses and structures not compatible with district objectives.
 The R-2 District permits a maximum residential building height of 40 feet above Design Flood Elevation (DFE). The floor-to-floor height is required to be at minimum 10 feet tall. A minimum rear setback of 30 feet, or 30% of lot, is required, while a 0-foot or 5-foot side yard setback is required. No front yard setback is required, except where prevailing setbacks apply. In such cases, the front yard prevailing setback can not exceed 10 feet. The number of residential units (i.e., density) allowed is determined by dividing the site area by 660 and rounding to the closest whole number. No off-street parking is required for the first five dwelling units. At least one space for each dwelling unit after the first five is required, except as modified in Article XI .
